Selected work

Three case studies

Anonymized stories from real projects. Open one to see how I think.

Context. I was the lead tester for a complex automated authorization feature in an operational system.

Challenge. Covering state transitions, event sequences, dependent entities and schedule changes while the process was live.

My approach. I used predefined API requests to simulate events and checked the behavior against the UI and the data with SQL.

Impact. The tests exposed defects, edge cases and undefined business rules, which led to clarifications in the specification.

Context. A nightly process that changes entities according to their status and effective dates.

Challenge. Understanding how one change affects other parts of the system.

My approach. I mapped the links between statuses, operational segments, schedules, active processes and dependent entities.

Impact. I designed test and regression combinations aimed at catching unexpected effects elsewhere in the system.

Context. Release testing on a system with many connected processes.

Challenge. The change looked contained, but its reach was wider than it seemed.

My approach. I widened my testing beyond the direct change and found an impact on schedule processing.

Impact. It created incorrect operational time data. The release was stopped, the defect fixed and retested, and the release continued once verified.
