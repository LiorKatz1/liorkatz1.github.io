Complex Operational Workflow

I owned QA for an automated authorization workflow, building coverage from scratch across state changes, dependent entities and live schedule changes. Testing uncovered defects and undefined business rules that led to clearer requirements.

Test designAPISQL
Read case study

Context

An automated authorization process connected sequential operational entities, real-time events, schedules and system states. I was its primary QA owner.

Challenge

The core flow was documented, but many combinations arising in real operations were still undefined.

My approach

I built scenario coverage from scratch, tested state transitions and event sequences, explored dependent entities and schedule changes during active processes, simulated events with predefined API requests, and checked UI behavior and data with SQL.

Impact

My testing exposed defects and edge cases. Questions I raised with System Analysts helped refine business rules and the feature specification.

Cross-System Impact Analysis

I tested a nightly process that updated operational entities by status and effective date. I mapped dependencies and designed combinations to catch effects beyond the changed feature.

IntegrationRegressionRisk analysis
Read case study

Context

A scheduled process updated operational entities according to status and effective dates.

Challenge

One update could affect operational segments, schedules, active processes and dependent business objects.

My approach

I mapped the relationships between affected areas and designed tests combining statuses, dates, active dependencies and restrictions. I included regression checks for connected workflows.

Impact

The resulting coverage accounted for possible effects on existing operational data beyond the process itself.

A Regression Caught Before Release

During release validation, I tested beyond the immediate change and found an interaction with schedule processing that created invalid operational time data. The release went ahead after the issue was fixed and retested.

RegressionRelease validation
Read case study

Context

I was validating a release involving a change to a connected operational system.

Challenge

A problem outside the immediate change could still compromise operational data.

My approach

I expanded regression checks to related schedule processing and identified invalid time data.

Impact

The deployment was stopped, the defect was fixed, and I retested the affected flow before the release proceeded.
